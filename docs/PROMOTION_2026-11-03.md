## Promoted: San Francisco Trolleybuses (23/25)

SFMTA’s current fleet page identifies the still-operating New Flyer trolleybus fleet as the largest in the United States and Canada. Its adopted FY2027–FY2031 capital programme also schedules replacement trolleybuses for revenue service. The claim is deliberately limited to current San Francisco operation; it does not claim global prevalence. Modern buses may bridge interruptions off-wire using batteries.
