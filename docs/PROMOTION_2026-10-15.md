# Collection review — 15 October 2026

San Francisco cable cars are exhibit 015 (23/25). SFMTA’s current Powell–Mason and Powell–Hyde pages mark both routes active, publish daily service hours and expose live stop data. The entry distinguishes the underground moving-cable system from electric streetcars, trolleybuses and aerial gondolas, and does not treat temporary maintenance interruptions as extinction.

Bookmobiles are the new provisional candidate (23/25: recognition 4, visual strength 5, story strength 4, survival evidence 5, catalogue fit 5). Mobile Public Library publishes an August–December 2026 schedule and documents borrowing, holds, programmes and public Wi-Fi aboard the vehicle. The claim is scoped to a verified active local service rather than every historic bookmobile programme.

Result: 15 live exhibits and 14 candidates. Local data, candidate, website and Saved State checks must pass, followed by all OG/X render reviews, browser website CI and a green pull-request workflow before merge.
