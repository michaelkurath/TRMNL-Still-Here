# Typewriters artwork — 2 November 2026

## Scope

Original interpretive artwork for exhibit 031. It depicts a generic maintained manual machine as the recognisable typewriter form; it is not a portrait of a Swintec model and does not claim that the pictured design is newly manufactured.

## Generation prompt

> Use case: illustration-story. Asset: TRMNL STILL HERE museum exhibit. Create an original high-contrast monochrome museum engraving of a traditional mechanical typewriter as a current maintained object. Show one complete open, unbranded typewriter in three-quarter view on a quiet white studio surface, with paper loaded. Keep the mechanism mechanically plausible. Fine black ink linework, restrained crosshatching, broad grayscale separation, no pre-dithering. Keep the entire machine, carriage ends, platen knobs, feet, keyboard and paper fully inside the centered 4:3 safe zone with at least 15% breathing room. No readable text on paper or keys, logos, brands, hands, people, extra machines, border or watermark.
