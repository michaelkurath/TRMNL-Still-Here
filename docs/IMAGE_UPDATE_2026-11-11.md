Pure black ink outlines on a pure white untextured background, like a crisp early patent illustration without labels. Use sparse thin parallel hatching only inside the object, NO broad solid black filled panels, NO mid-gray shading, no cast shadow, no reflection, no paper grain. Keep body and base mostly white with dark outline and sparse visible detail. Entire object including crank on a wide canvas with white margins. No hands, treadle, motor, branding, borders, arrows, text or watermark. Not a portrait of a specific product.
